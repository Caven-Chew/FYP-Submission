Local static target and saved-judge calls are recorded here, including archived executions and interrupted calls not yet attached to a result. Multi-turn attacks record three stages per invocation, each linked to its saved trajectory: target_send (a send to the target), attacker_call (the attacker model, including Crescendo's refusal check and TAP's on-topic check) and judge_internal (PyRIT's own scorer calls that steer the attack). SDK retries inside one invocation, converter calls and Azure scans are not counted. Missing history means unknown, not zero calls; this is not a billing record.

Static send completion time includes retries and backoff, minus recorded throttle rest. Judge evaluation time includes its retries and waits. The invocation durations below exclude this helper's backoff, but may include SDK or browser work.
